Load a visualization, evaluate it using our three-item rubric with detailed comments, and save your comprehensive review.

How to Use the Demo

Follow these steps to evaluate a visualization:

  • Step 1: Upload an image by dragging & dropping, using the file upload button, or pasting a URL
  • Step 2: Rate the visualization on three criteria: Lie Factor, Data-Ink Ratio, and Chart Junk
  • Step 3: Add detailed comments for each criterion to explain your evaluation
  • Step 4: Click "Save Review" to store your feedback

Our enhanced interface uses nudges to guide you toward more comprehensive reviews.
